# EduProlog roadmap

EduProlog takes a learner from no prior logic-programming experience to confident practical Prolog.

## Learning path

### Part I — Foundations
0. First contact: facts, rules, queries and backtracking
1. Terms and unification
2. Relations and recursion
3. Lists and recursive data
4. Arithmetic and comparisons

### Part II — How Prolog executes
5. Search, choice points and goal order
6. Negation and the closed-world assumption
7. Cut, if-then-else and controlled search
8. Input/output and modules

### Part III — Building reliable programs
9. Testing with plunit
10. Debugging, tracing and common mistakes
11. Higher-order predicates and common library patterns
12. Trees, graphs and search problems

### Part IV — Symbolic applications
13. Definite Clause Grammars (DCG)
14. Parsing and simple natural-language processing
15. Constraint logic programming with CLP(FD)
16. Knowledge representation and inference
17. Small expert systems

### Part V — Advanced Prolog
18. Meta-programming and interpreters
19. Performance, indexing and termination
20. Portability and ISO Prolog
21. Interfacing Prolog with other systems
22. Capstone project

Each module should contain explanation, runnable examples, exercises, tests, a challenge and a short reflection section. Norwegian is the primary edition; English is maintained alongside it.
